Optional: put your own Crash Team Racing Nitro-Fueled PS4 package here (one
game .pkg; a fake/debug PKG, as retail packages are encrypted; tested: CUSA13795,
USA) to get an "NF" version of every track in the native build. Its update
package (one; tested: v1.21) may go here too: it adds the Grand Prix tracks.
They are not included and must never be shared. scripts/build_native.sh unpacks
them (needs the .NET 8 SDK and about 50 GB) and converts their levels.
Nitro-Fueled only tracks (Crash Nitro Kart's, the Grand Prix ones) are offered
when a fan made PS1 remake of them is installed (tracks_in/; see
scripts/nf_tracks.py HOSTED): the remake is played, the Nitro-Fueled level drawn.
